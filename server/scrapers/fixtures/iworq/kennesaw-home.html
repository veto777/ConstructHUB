Residential Building Permit

Apply Here

PERMITS ARE: NON-TRANSFERABLE & NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS NEEDED BEFORE ISSUANCE OF PERMIT:

*** IF YOUR PROPERTY IS IN THE HISTORIC DISTRICT A CERTIFICATE OF APPROPRIATENESS MUST BE APPROVED PRIOR TO APPLYING FOR A BUILDING PERMIT. THIS INCLUDES ANY EXTERIOR WORK.

>*** IF THERE IS NO APPROVAL ON FILE YOUR PERMIT APPLICATION WILL BE DENIED.

>***If you are unsure whether the proposed work requires a Certificate of Appropriateness, please contact Planning and Zoning at 770-590-8268.

***New house plans must be drawn to scale***

  1. Certificate of Appropriateness (If located within a historic district)
  2. Apply for Land-Disturbance Permit (separate submittal)
  3. 1 Complete sets of the house/building plans in PDF Uploaded
  4. House location plan (Example)
  5. Paid water receipt Copy from Cobb County Water & Sewer
  6. Contractors current (inside the state of GA) business license
  7. Contractors current state license

**Deck (Repair, Replace and New)

  1. Building plans or Manufacturer’s Specifications (must show detail about the type of exterior material)
  2. Site Plan Indicating distance of existing and/or new deck from all property lines
  3. Floor plan with dimensions showing the layout of the deck
  4. Show size and length of lumber used
  5. Identify modifications to any existing structure: post, beams joist, etc.
  6. Indicate height of deck from ground, and showing footing specs

**Retaining Wall (Anything over 48 inches from bottom of footing up needs engineer design plans and requires a permit)

  1. Site Plan showing wall/s on property with specs
  2. If concrete block engineered system, wall must be built according to the manufactures spec that come with the system
  3. Land disturbance permit is needed if a machine will be used to excavate dirt

**Basement Finish

  1. Floor plan with room identification, room dimensions and window sizes with any additional work. Electrical, Plumbing and HVAC.
  2. Wall Section showing type of wall, to be constructed if any
  3. Energy code affidavit posted per code. (HVAC)

**Enclosed Garage

  1. Site Plan/ Survey, showing distance to all property lines for enclosed garage
  2. Floor plan with room identification, room dimensions and window sizes with any additional work.
  3. Wall Section showing type of wall, to be constructed if any
  4. Energy code affidavit posted per code. (HVAC)

**Additions/Sunrooms/Screened Porches

  1. Site Plan showing the location of the addition with the distance from all property lines
  2. Floor Plan with details of structure, including the square footage, materials used, height, width etc..
  3. Wall section showing wall from footing to roof-tie in, and with window sizes if applicable

**Sheds & Carports

  1. Building plans drawn to scale or Manufacturer’s Specifications (plans must include details about the type of exterior material, roof material and must include details about the amount of impervious surface coverage on the property)
  2. Site plan which shows the location of the proposed structure, location of the primary structure and shows the distance (in feet) from proposed shed or carport to the property line
  3. Certificate of Appropriateness (If located within a historic district)

Code Complaint

Click Here

Thank you for using our Report a Concern Online Notification System. Please be as detailed as possible as to the issue of concern: the property address of concern; description of what is wrong; and your contact information (including a good time to call). All complaints submitted to the Community Development Department are subject to the Georgia Open Records Act. If an Open Records request is filed, the complaint information, including emails, will be provided to the person requesting the information, unless exempted by State law. No pictures are necessary as our Code Inspectors must observe the violation to take action.

This link is for Private Property Issues only. Other agencies to contact for concerns include:

  • Issues related to illegal activity/law enforcement issues – Police Department 770-422-2505 or 911 for Emergencies.

  • Street issues (lights, maintenance, etc.) – Public Works Department 770-421-8582

  • Gas leaks – Dial 911 for Emergency Response

  • Water/sanitary sewer issues - Cobb County Water; emergencies 770-419-6201 and 770-423-1000 Customer Service.

The Code Enforcement division is dedicated to enhancing the quality of life for the citizens of Kennesaw by providing effective enforcement of City adopted ordinances, housing codes, and zoning codes.


The Code Enforcement Division handles private property issues including off street parking (contact the police patrol division concerning on street parking issues). The City encourages citizens to participate in keeping Kennesaw a clean, safe place to live and raise families by voluntarily complying with codes and reporting possible violations, particularly in these areas:

  • Junk vehicles

  • Overgrown yards or lots

  • Illegal dumping

  • Graffiti

  • Illegal signs

  • Dilapidated housing

  • Immediate Safety Concerns (abandoned refrigerators, unsecured pools, aggressive/vicious animals, etc.)

The City Code of Ordinances are available at:

https://library.municode.com/ga/kennesaw/codes/code_of_ordinances
