* Property Information

A property is required to submit this application. A matching property must be selected from the search results for it to be included on the application.

Contractor(s)

A contractor is required to submit this application. A matching contractor must be selected from the search results for it to be included on the application.

Step 1: Search for and select your project address using the above "Property Information" search box. Then add the Owner information using the boxes below to enter the updated information.

Step 2: Provide contractor information in the boxes below for each applicable type of contractor working on your project. If you are acting as an owner-builder, please type “owner-builder” in the General Contractor boxes below. If you have yet to select a contractor, please type “TBD” in the General Contractor boxes below. Note: Contractor information is required prior to permit issuance

Step 3: Applicant Information

Step 4: Project Information

When estimating project valuation, include both material and labor costs. This will determine your non-refundable plan check and building permit fees.

Step 5: Please upload your plans in PDF format using the "Choose Files" button below. If your plans are too large to attach, please email them with your project address to permits@ch.utah.gov

Step 6: Digital Signature. Please read the acknowledgements below and type your name into the signature box.

By signing below, you acknowledge that your permit remains pending and will not be reviewed until the appropriate fees are paid. Fees vary from project to project, and will be calculated by staff. After initial processing of your application is complete, staff will send an online payment link to the permit applicant. The applicant can then pay the relevant fees using the online payment portal.
