Click icon to search Permits

Summary

Here you can view existing permit information, request inspections, resubmit plans, upload additional documentation, or pay outstanding fees.

A Contractor Access Code is required to schedule inspections and upload files. This number is typically your Georgia State Contractor’s License Number. For other specialty contractors this number is your business license/occupational tax certificate number. For property owners who are acting as the general contractor, the code will be the numeric portion of the project address.

**For Commercial Business Owners attempting to upload a "Life Safety Floor Plan" the Code is 0000**

NOTE: We do not accept same day inspection requests. Please click in Requested Date option to select the future date you are requesting.

If you need assistance, please contact the Planning and Development Department at chambleedevelopment@chambleega.gov

Accessory Structure Application

Accessory Structure

This application is for accessory structures. Accessory structures are “subordinate and incidental” to a principal or main structure on the same property. For example, a single-family house would be a principle structure, and a shed, detached carport or garage, or playground set would be an accessory structure. Accessory structures are not attached to the principal structure. Use this application to submit detached garage/shed/carport, pool/spa, fence, and any other related permits.

Please upload the following documents (each file must be under 50 MB)
1 – Site Plan
2 – Construction Details
3 – Floor Plan
4 – Current Contractor Licenses

Zoning Verification For Business License

Click Icon to Apply

This application is for zoning verification for occupational tax certificate (OTC) business license purpose only. This is the first step in the business license process to ensure that the new use is permitted according to the zoning for the location.

This application is required for new businesses, existing businesses with a change of ownership, change of location, change of use, adding additional business use, and/or change of business name.

This application must be submitted and approved before applying for an OTC (occupational tax certificate). The application will ask for a “Contractor”. Please enter New Business as the default contractor.

Instructions

1
Click the icon on the left to start application process
2
Property address at the top is required
3
Type New Business in the Contractor box.
4
Complete all fields
5
