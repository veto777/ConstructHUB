Online Permitting

(Instructions are in the drop-downs below)

Welcome to our online permitting portal; hard-copy permit applications are not accepted. Our portal is administered by MUNIS. We will not issue a permit until all permit fees are paid in full. Our system generates and delivers the permit, so please make sure you provide a valid email address. 

For those that need assistance and to schedule inspections, please feel free to contact David Evans at 603-659-8501, ext. 1311 or email David Evans or Bridgette Grotheer at 603-659-8501, ext. 1310 or email Bridgette Grotheer.

Please note: When searching for an address, please only use the street address. Because the owner's name is pulled from our tax database, it may not match what is entered and will result in the system not finding the property. 

PLEASE READ ALL INSTRUCTIONS PROVIDED IN THE BELOW DROP-DOWNS BEFORE APPLYING OR PAYING FOR A PERMIT.

  1. APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS & WEBSITE

Click here to apply for a permit.

  • *Please only enter the street number and street name; leave the owner's name and parcel ID blank.* 

  • Please also leave off the street suffix (Street, Avenue, Lane, etc.) See below image for an example.

  1. PAYMENT INSTRUCTIONS & WEBSITE

Read below for steps to pay for a permit. *NEW PORTAL*

  • To pay for the permit via the website, the easiest way is to follow these steps:
    • Go to https://townofnewmarketnh.tylerportico.com/css/citizen-selfservice-bl/permit-inspection/search.
    • Click "Advanced search":

  • Search by street number and street name (either leave off or abbreviate street suffix (Rd, St, etc.)), or search by application ID only if known:

  • Once you click on the correct application, click the "Fees" tab at the top, and "Pay fee," which will add it to the cart.
  • If paying for multiple permits, repeat steps, and click on the cart in the top right corner when ready to check out.
