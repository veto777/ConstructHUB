Search Permits

How to Search for a Permit

You can use the online permit search to look up specific projects by project details, property address, or contractor.

Step 1 — Open the Permitting & Registration Page

Step 2 — Select “Search Permits”
(On the left side of the page, locate the navigation menu and click Search Permits.)

Step 3 — Log In or Create an Account
You will be prompted to log in to your account.
(If you do not already have an account, select New Account and follow the instructions to create one.)

Step 4 — Enter Your Search Information

Once logged in, you can search for a permit using any of the available information, including:
- Project details
- Property address
- Contractor
(Enter the information you have available. You do not need to know every project detail to perform a search.)

Step 5 — Select “Search”
After entering your search information, scroll to the bottom of the page and click Search.

Step 6 — Select the Project
Projects matching the information you entered will appear in the search results at the bottom of the page.
Locate the project you want to view, click the three dots (⋯) next to the project, and select View Project.
You will then be able to view the available permit and project information.
