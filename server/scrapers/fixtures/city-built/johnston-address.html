View Permits by Address

The Johnston County Building Inspections Department is pleased to provide you with the ability to view building inspections permits by address.

Notes:

(1)  Building Permits are available online for the previous six (6) years. Please contact the Building Inspections department directly for records older than six years.
(2)  This data is generated directly from the Inspections Department's records. Please be patient while your results load. Thank you.

Find Building Inspections Permits by Address

  • Only enter the complete street name (e.g., Main, Lee, etc.). Do not include your street direction (e.g., N, S, E, W). Do not include your street type (e.g., Drive, Road, Street, etc.).
  • Enter numbers only in the House Number field. Do not enter letters, dashes, or any non-numeric character in this field.
