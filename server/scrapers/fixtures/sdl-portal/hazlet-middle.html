Property Simple Search
  • To find a specific property, search by the full address of the property including any street numbers.
  • For the best results, include the street name but exclude the street type (i.e. search for "Main" and not "Main St" or "Main Street")
  • Use the Advanced Search option when you do not have complete address information OR you are searching for several properties at once by searching by Block, Lot, owner, location name or street name.
Permit Simple Search
  • To find a specific permit, search by the full or partial permit or control number or by a specific street address
  • For permit or control numbers, be sure to include any non-alphanumeric characters in your query
  • For the best results, include the street name but exclude the street type (i.e. search for "Main" and not "Main St" or "Main Street")
  • The tips below are useful for keyword searches.
    • 13-02748+A - Exact Permit Or Control Number searches
    • spring or springfield - All permits on Springfield Dr.
    • pn:13-0274 - Search Permit Numbers starting with '13-0274'
    • cn:13-02 - Search Control Numbers starting with '13-02'
  • Use the Advanced Search option if you are searching for multiple permit based on keywords, permit issued dates, user groups, or street location
  • Note: Not all permits may be available on the portal. Please contact your town official if you are unable to locate a permit.
Property Advanced Search
  • Include at least one value in the available search options.
  • For the best results in street searches, you many need to exclude the street type (i.e. search for "Main" and not "Main St" or "Main Street").
  • For the best results for property owner searches, use the "Last Name, First Name" format.
Permits Advanced Search
  • Include at least one value in the available search options.
  • For keyword search, up to three comma separated keywords can be included
  • For the best results, include the street name but exclude the street type (i.e. search for "Main" and not "Main St" or "Main Street") for location related searches
