City of Tamarac | Building Permits & Inspections Portal

🔧 Online Building Permits & Contractor Services

Our ePermits portal makes it easy for citizens and contractors to complete the most common steps in the building permit process online. This includes:

  • Submitting applications
  • Paying permit fees
  • Scheduling inspections
  • Viewing inspection results
  • Searching permit history

📘 For detailed instructions, please consult the ePermits Applicant Guide.


🛠️ Contractor Registration & Updates

All contractors must be registered with the City of Tamarac before they can be selected for a permit application.

There is no fee to register.

  ➤ New Contractor Registration

New contractors must complete the full registration process by submitting all required information and documentation via the online form:
🔗 Register as a Contractor

The Building Department will review your submission and notify you once your registration is approved.

  🔁 Already Registered? Submit Updates by Email

If you are an existing registered contractor, please do not use the full registration form to submit updates.

For updates such as renewed licenses or insurance documents, simply email the new documents to:
📧 Contractor@Tamarac.gov

Note: Please only send updated or expiring documents - do not resend the entire registration packet.
