## Why the first numbers were wrong
The preview showed "560 quality". zillow.com, raspberrypi.com and news.bloomberglaw.com were "A 100" guest-post sites:
the grader counted any page whose TEXT said "write for us" (an article, a spammer's zillow.com profile titled "Guest
Posting") and then added +25 for domain strength, so big brands topped the list. Many others were guest-post *lists*,
link sellers, job boards, SEO agencies and foreign sites.

## The rules now (server/backlinks/vet.ts — `gradeSource`)
- The SITE must invite submissions: a page whose title/heading/address is the invitation, or a same-site link to one
  (`submissionUrl`). A phrase in an article or on a user profile does not count.
- Guest post / resource / editorial: the site's HOME page must be about home improvement / construction
  (`siteOnTopic`; trade vocabulary only — "home", "window", "install" alone no longer count).
- Rejected: platforms (`EXCLUDED_DOMAINS`, now incl. zillow, realtor, yelp, homeadvisor, angi, thumbtack, bbb, substack…),
  guest-post lists, link-building sellers/case studies, job boards, SEO/marketing agencies, foreign ccTLDs and foreign
  cities/countries in the home title.
- Free blog sub-domains (wordpress.com, blogspot, wixsite, *.home.blog…) max C; a site that blocks the crawler max C
  whatever its authority. A = 70+ and needs measured authority.
- All 978 old sources were re-queued (tier cleared) and 263 unsent drafts deleted (none approved or sent).
Result: old database 55 quality (39 A, 16 B) instead of 540.
