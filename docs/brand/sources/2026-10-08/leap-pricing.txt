Frequently Asked Questions
Do you charge extra for integrations?
No. Leap does not charge extra to use any of our integrated partners. You will only pay for the third-party services themselves.
How long are the contracts?
All Leap plans are available in one-year contracts paid monthly.
How often will I be billed?
Billing is processed monthly on the date you signed up.
Can I upgrade my Leap plan?
Yes. If you sign up for Leap CRM Essential and decide to upgrade to Team, just reach out to our support team and they will take care of it.
Does Leap offer a free trial?
Yes. Leap CRM Essential includes a 14-day free trial so you can get started before committing.
