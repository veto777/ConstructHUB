Frequently asked questions
What's included in each plan, and are there extra costs?
Basic covers the essentials, Essentials adds tools for growing teams, and MAX is built to scale. Add-ons and extra users are optional, so you only pay for what you need.
Am I locked into a contract with Housecall Pro?
No, unlike some other field service management software, Housecall Pro does not require long-term contracts. Cancelling is simple and hassle-free. For more information, visit our
Help Center
or call us at 858-251-9751.
Who is Housecall Pro for?
Housecall Pro is designed for home service businesses like HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, landscaping, and others. Whether you’re a solo operator or have a growing team, our platform has the features to support your success.
Can I accept payments through Housecall Pro?
Yes! Securely accept credit and debit card payments on invoices and in the field. Deposit checks for no fee through the mobile app. You can also accept bank payments and offer consumer financing to your customers. A 1% fee is applied to bank payments, and card processing fees start at 2.59%.
Is Housecall Pro a fit for franchisees?
Absolutely! We have special offers designed for franchises and businesses with multiple locations.
Contact us today
to explore customized pricing and solutions built for your needs.
How does the free trial work?
Enjoy full access to all Housecall Pro features on our MAX plan for 14 days, absolutely free—no credit card needed to start! We’ll send you a friendly reminder before your trial ends to easily add your billing details without interruption. Even if your trial expires, your data is safe, and you can resume anytime by entering your billing information.
Will Housecall Pro work with other software?
Yes, Housecall Pro’s
field service management software
integrates with popular business tools such as QuickBooks, Google, and Thumbtack, among others. For even more flexibility, our MAX plan offers access to Zapier and our open API, allowing you to create custom integrations to fit your specific workflows.
How flexible are Housecall Pro plans?
We offer flexible plans to suit your business needs. You can upgrade anytime for immediate access to more features. And you can also downgrade anytime with changes taking effect at the end of your current monthly or annual billing period.
Do you offer Housecall Pro for Spanish-speaking teams?
Yes! Housecall Pro is available in Spanish to support our Spanish-speaking Pros. Our mobile app includes a Spanish language option, and our dedicated Spanish-speaking support team is here to help you every step of the way.
