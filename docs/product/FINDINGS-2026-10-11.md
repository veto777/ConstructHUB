## SEO Suite (found while seeding sample data, 2026-10-11)
1. `configured:false` (no DataForSEO key) puts "Rank tracking is being switched on for your account — check back
   shortly" at the top of EVERY SEO page, including pages that spend nothing (Site audit, Local grid, Reports,
   Keywords explorer); the rank tracker's "Next automatic check" tile reads "— Being switched on". Scope the notice
   to the pages that need the provider.
2. Two "Competitors" destinations with different meanings: `/seo/competitors` (paid competitor gap) vs the
   Rank-tracker nav entry → `/seo/rank-tracker?panel=competitors` (share of voice). Name them apart.
3. Alerts page opens on the keyword-watch "Searches newly seen" panel; the alert list is below the fold even with
   10 alerts. Alerts first.
4. Saved-report windows are short and inconsistent (content/planner/directories 24 h; keyword overviews, mentions,
   explorer reports 7 d). A planner table rerun 9 days ago shows "Nothing has been charged yet / Get the numbers"
   although the usage ledger lists the lookup.
5. Backlinks header says "Domain rank 238, 0–1000" while the dashboard/explorer call the same figure
   "Authority 24, 0–100". One scale.
6. Planner view URL is `?view=area` but nothing in the UI says so; the keywords-explorer tab param differs from the
   `tab=` convention elsewhere.
7. Local grid rivals table: "In the first 3" counts every point where the rival appeared anywhere in that point's
   top three, so a rival can show "25 of 49" beside our own 4.4 position score. Heading should say what it counts.
8. Rank-tracker "Since last check ▲27 ▼7" counts the primary device only while the table shows both devices;
   not labelled.
9. Content explorer cache key includes `exclude: site.domain` when "Leave out <site>" is ticked (default), so a
   search run unticked is "Not searched yet" when reopened ticked.
10. Dashboard "Average site health 100 — of the 1 crawled site" with 3 sites; uncrawled cards show "—" with an
    "Open Site audit" button that lands on an empty audit. Offer "Run first crawl" instead.
